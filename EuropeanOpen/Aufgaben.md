  ### Infos Poster (Veranstalter):
  1. Poster Size – at most A1 Size 70.7cm (28”) high x 100.0cm (40”) wide (landscape).

  2. Title / Identification – team name, region, sub-league.

  3. Abstract – A concise summary of the entire project. The abstract should summarize the critical elements of the poster, but should avoid repeating what is          stated   elsewhere in the poster.

  4. Method / Robot Production / Design – A description of the choices made during the robots’ production, including the  
     rationale underlying those choices. Production includes the design, construction, programming, component selection, and overall process. Teams should              indicate the programming language, sensors used, time and cost of development. Also to include a bill of materials for the major components.

  5. Data / Results / Discussion - The poster has details of the team’s development and testing of the robot including any relevant data and modifications made as      part of the robot’s creation.

  6. Photos / Images – All images, including graphics for styling, should either be original or available for non-commercial reuse with modification  
     as per thecreative commons license (http://creativecommons.org/). Any photo or image should be labeled and cited especially if not original.

  7. All information in the poster should be in English.
